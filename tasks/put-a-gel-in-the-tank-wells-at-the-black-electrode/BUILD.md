# Building the rig

A stock horizontal mini-gel system can be used instead (any 10 x 7 cm tray and tank with red/black leads, for example a
Bio-Rad Mini-Sub Cell GT class system). The simulated rig is one made from the parts below; sizes are in mm.

| Part | Size / material | Qty |
|---|---|---|
| Tank floor | 150 x 300 x 3 cast acrylic, clear | 1 |
| Tank side walls | 300 x 60 x 3 acrylic | 2 |
| End caps (electrode housings) | 150 x 12 x 60 acrylic, one red, one black | 2 |
| Gel deck | 60 x 160 x 20 acrylic, solvent-welded to the floor | 1 |
| Electrode | 130 mm platinum-coated or stainless wire, 0.5 mm, on two 6 mm posts | 2 |
| Terminals | 4 mm banana jacks, one red, one black, wired to the electrodes | 2 |
| Gel tray | 100 x 70 x 3 floor with 2 mm walls 12 mm tall on the long sides, open ends, UV-transparent acrylic | 1 |
| Alignment marks | black tape 3 mm wide across the deck, 115 mm apart (inside edges) | 2 |
| Gel | 1 percent agarose, cast 6 mm thick in the tray with an 8-well comb (5 mm teeth) | 1 per trial |

Assembly: weld walls, end caps and deck to the floor with acrylic cement, seat the electrode posts and wires near each
end cap and wire them to the jacks, stick the tape marks on the deck. Cast the gel in the tray with its ends dammed
(tape or gaskets), remove the dams once set, then place the tray on the table as in the task setup. Approx. cost: tank
and tray about 60 USD in acrylic, or a stock system around 300 USD.
