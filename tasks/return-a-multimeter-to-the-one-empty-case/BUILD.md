# Building the cases

Three identical cases (about 20 USD each as printed ABS or cut acrylic; or any hard case with a sliding lid of the
same size):

- Base: 14.5 cm (front to back) x 20 cm (wide) x 5.4 cm tall, 5 mm walls, black ABS. Inside: a 11.5 x 17 cm recess,
  4.4 cm deep from the rim, with a 1 cm foam floor (EVA foam, dark grey).
- Lid: 14.5 x 20 x 0.8 cm ABS, dark grey, sliding in a groove along the two long sides, travel 15.5 cm toward the back
  (stop at the end of the groove). Glue a 3 x 4 cm, 2.6 cm tall grey ABS finger knob on the lid's front edge. Slide
  friction should be light (about 2-5 N).
- Multimeters: three identical handheld digital multimeters about 15 x 7.5 x 3.6 cm (yellow rubber holster); dead or
  dummy units are fine. No probe leads.

Mark the returned meter's start position on the bench (12 cm out, on the midline, long side across the bench).
